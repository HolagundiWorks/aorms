Every piece of office software you've used probably ties your account to one employer. Change firms, and you lose the login, the history, everything you built up — your project experience, your certifications, whatever reputation the system tracked, all of it stays behind with the company that issued the account. That's fine for payroll software. It's a genuine loss for anything that's supposed to represent *you* as a professional, not just your current employer's records about you.

AORMS Identity exists because an architect's career doesn't reset every time they change firms, and their software account shouldn't either.

## One handle, not one login per employer

An AORMS Identity is a portable personal account — an `AORMS-U-` handle that belongs to you, not to any one studio. Link it once, and you carry the same identity into every practice you work at, present or future. A studio's Office Hub login gets you into that studio's clients, projects, and files. Your AORMS Identity is separate from all of that — it's yours, independent of who currently employs you.

This isn't a hypothetical convenience. Architects change firms, go independent, come back into practice, consult across multiple studios simultaneously. A portable identity means none of that requires starting over.

## Earned, not applied for

Every AORMS Identity starts at a Basic level. Cross 100 hours of active use, and it promotes to Pro automatically — no application form, no approval step, nothing to request. It's a recognition of real, accumulated usage, not a paywall or a sales gate. You don't ask for it. You just cross the threshold and it's there.

We built it this way deliberately. A lot of "leveling up" systems in business software exist to create friction — an upsell dressed up as a milestone. This one doesn't gate any feature behind Pro; it's simply an honest signal, visible on your own identity, of how much real usage sits behind it.

## Licensing lives here too

A studio's own licence — Trial, Standard, or Premium, how many seats, when it renews — sits under the same Identity sign-in that manages your personal account. If you're a studio owner, this is also where you manage what your practice is actually paying for, in one place, not scattered across a billing portal that has nothing to do with your actual login.
