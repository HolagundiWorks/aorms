AORMS marketing and product chrome now share **pure neumorphism** from
`@hcw/ui-kit` **1.5.0**. Soft raised and recessed surfaces carry the UI —
not translucent glass panels.

## What changed

- **Opaque soft materials only** — light, dark, and high-contrast schemes keep
  the same neu recipes; glass recipes alias to soft.
- **8px radius** on docks, buttons, and cards — square-ish, not capsules.
- **Marketing top ribbon** replaces the left SoftRail on Home, Blog, and
  Downloads; a fixed **analogue clock** sits bottom-right (separate from any
  digital tray clock in the staff workspace).
- **Radiant Orange `#FF4F18`** remains the single accent; fills use white text.

## Why

Depth encodes importance without theatrical glass. Consultancies read calm,
tactile surfaces better than competing translucent layers — especially on the
desktop-preferred local-first node where the shell and SPA share one look.
