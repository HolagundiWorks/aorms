**AStudio** serves architecture consultancies. **AConsulting** serves engineering
firms. Indian **project management consultancies** — the extended arm of the
developer or owner — need a different workspace: programme oversight, package
tenders, and certification of contractor bills, not another Gantt that pretends
to replace Primavera.

**AProc** (*Accelerated Project Management*) is that workspace. It is in
**preview** at `proc.aorms.in`, on the same AORMS spine as the other two apps.

## What AProc is (and is not)

AProc is **owner-side / PMC-side governance**:

- Master **programme** milestones for client reporting (import **CSV** or
  **Primavera P6 XER** milestones — not a full CPM engine)
- **Package** register with sealed **contractor portal** bids (the PMC firm does
  not bid as a contractor)
- **RA bill** and **steel** certification for the client
- **BBS** (IS 456 cutting lengths) and **steel reconciliation** by diameter
- Site supervision already shared with Studio — snags, progress reports, visits

AProc is **not** contractor labour payroll, plant hire ERP, or a rebuild of
P6. Planners keep MSP/P6; AProc imports the milestones the PMC must report and
certify against.

## Same spine

Identity, licensing, portals, EOMS, and ESTI sit on the platform. PMC firms get
a discipline-fit chrome (`/pmc` home, Delivery-weighted projects) without a
forked stack.
