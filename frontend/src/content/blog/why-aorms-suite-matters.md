Consulting offices do not fail because they lack software. They fail because **too many tools own a piece of the same project** — and none of them own the whole story.

Messaging holds the decision. A sheet holds the fee. A CAD file holds the geometry. Email holds the approval. By the time someone asks “what did we bill for this revision?” the answer lives in three inboxes and one principal’s memory.

**AORMS** (*Accelerated Operational Resources Management System*) is built for that gap. It is a **product suite for AEC consulting and PMC firms** — architecture, engineering, and project management practices that **advise and govern**, not contractor labour ERPs.

## Why a suite — not one mega-app

A single browser ERP that tries to do BOQ math, bar bending, drafting, HR, and client portals in one tab always collapses into either:

- a slow cloud CAD that practices will not trust with drafts, or
- an office tool that cannot price steel or measure a BOQ correctly.

So AORMS splits the work the way firms already split people:

| Plane | What lives there | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Practice managers** | Tasks, office, HR, payroll views, portal publish | Communications and commercial rhythm |
| **Technical apps** | Estimation, BBS, programme / RA | Heavy calc stays local on a shared engine |
| **Drafting / geometry** | AADT + ShilpiDB | Drawings are not “files in chat” |
| **Online** | Firm portals + hub | Clients see **published** updates only |

That split is the product. It is why the suite matters more than any single screen.

## What gets better when the suite is in place

**Fee recovery stops leaking into uninvoiced nights.** Revisions, proposals, and invoices stay attached to one project record in the practice manager — not a thread that died last Tuesday.

**Technical work stays trustworthy.** Estimation, BBS, and project management run on the desktop with a shared `bbs_engine`. The cloud never recomputes cutting lengths or BOQ lines. Portals receive totals and issued PDFs — never draft geometry.

**Clients stop hunting for the “latest” drawing.** Firm-branded portals show Updates, Progress, Drawings, and Documents from published packages. Staff ERP is not on `aorms.in`; the apex site is marketing and the blog while desktop packaging ships.

**AI stays cited and local where it counts.** **EOMS** is the external knowledge bank for codes. **ESTI** answers from validated firm repositories on desktop managers — propose, never auto-commit money or CAD.

## Who it is for

- Architecture practices that lose margin to undocumented client changes
- Engineering consultancies that need the same office rhythm without absorbing BOQ into chat
- PMC firms that govern programme and site certification without becoming a contractor ERP
